Created by Ajay Pulikkal · @ajaycarlos

JotPay is an Android personal finance and expense-tracking application created by Ajay Pulikkal, an independent student developer known online as ajaycarlos.

The application is designed to help users record and organize personal financial activity. It is available on Google Play under the package identifier com.ajaydev.jotpay.
